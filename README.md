BodyBalance AI

Understand today. Support the next step.

A decision-support app for active people. It combines a daily check-in, a tap-to-mark Pain Locator, optional BodyScope AI posture analysis and your history to recommend one safe next step: maintain, modify, reduce intensity, recover, monitor, or seek professional evaluation.
